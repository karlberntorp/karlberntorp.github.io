I am a Principal Research Scientist at Chewy, leading autonomy algorithm strategy and development for mobile manipulation systems. Previously, I was an Algorithm Leader and Distinguished Scientist at Symbotic, an Algorithms Team Leader and Distinguished Scientist at Walmart Advanced Systems & Robotics, and a Senior Principal Researcher and Control for Autonomy Team Leader at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories.

My work has covered robotics, autonomous transportation, localization, estimation, and control. It includes research leadership, published methods, prototypes, and contributions carried through to production systems. My CV lists more than 120 peer-reviewed journal and conference papers and numerous granted US patents.

I earned a PhD in Automatic Control from Lund University in 2014 and an MSc in Engineering Physics there in 2008. I have served as an Associate Editor of *IEEE Transactions on Control Systems Technology* and on the inaugural Technology Conference Editorial Board for the *IEEE Conference on Control Technology and Applications*. I am a Senior Member of IEEE.
