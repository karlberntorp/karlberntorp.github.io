At Chewy, I lead autonomy algorithm strategy and development for a fleet of mobile manipulation systems. I guide cross-functional research from prototype through validation and engineering handoff, with a focus on reliable manipulation of heavy deformable items at scale.

My work also spans multi-agent orchestration for autonomous robot fleets, including task allocation, routing, and control. I build validation frameworks, lead cross-functional research programs, and guide algorithm improvements into deployed systems. I have managed and mentored PhD researchers, led multi-group industrial research, and founded or co-founded two engineering consultancies.
