At Chewy, I own autonomy algorithm strategy and execution for a fleet of mobile manipulation systems. I lead cross-functional R&D from prototype to engineering handoff, focused on reliable manipulation of heavy deformable items at scale.

Previously, at Walmart Advanced Systems & Robotics and Symbotic, I worked on multi-agent orchestration: task allocation, routing, and control for autonomous robot fleets. I helped carry algorithm improvements into production. At Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories, I led the Control for Autonomy team and research in autonomous transportation, localization, estimation, and control.

Across these roles, I have set strategy and success metrics, built validation frameworks, led cross-functional programs with business impact, and worked with senior leadership to scale algorithmic practices. I have managed and mentored PhD researchers and led multi-group industrial research. I earned my PhD in Automatic Control from Lund University.
